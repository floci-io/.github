# Support

Floci is a free, open-source project. Community support is available through the channels below.
There is no paid support tier required to use any emulated service.

## Where to get help

- 🗣️ **[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/floci-io/discussions)**: questions, feature
  ideas, design tradeoffs, and proposals. Best for anything that benefits from being searchable
  later.
- 💬 **[Slack](https://join.slack.com/t/floci/shared_invite/zt-3tjn02s3q-A00kEjJ1cZxsg_imTfy6Cw)**:
  real-time questions and community chat.
- 🐛 **Issues**: for reproducible bugs and concrete compatibility gaps, open an issue on the
  relevant repository:
  [AWS](https://github.com/floci-io/floci/issues) ·
  [Azure](https://github.com/floci-io/floci-az/issues) ·
  [GCP](https://github.com/floci-io/floci-gcp/issues) ·
  [OCI](https://github.com/floci-io/floci-oci/issues)
- 📖 **[Documentation](https://floci.io)**: quick-start guides, configuration, and per-service
  details.

## Scope of community support

Community support is provided on a best-effort basis by maintainers and other community members.
Every emulated service is available to every user, always, with **no feature gates and no paid unlocks**.
For security issues, do not use the public channels above; follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md)
instead.
